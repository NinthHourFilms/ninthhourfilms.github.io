Data deletion

Last updated: 12 November 2026

AI Cinema Studio is the private production tool of Ninth Hour Films. It connects only to our own accounts and keeps only our own production data on our own computer. It does not collect information about viewers or other people.

Remove the application's access

  • Google (YouTube, Google Drive): open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose AI Cinema Studio and remove its access.
  • Facebook: Settings and privacy › Settings › Apps and websites, then remove AI Cinema Studio.
  • Instagram: Settings › Apps and websites, then remove AI Cinema Studio.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app's settings, open the list of apps connected to your account and remove AI Cinema Studio.

Removing access stops the application from reaching the account at once.

Ask us to delete data

If you believe the application holds any information about you, contact us through the Ninth Hour Films YouTube channel at youtube.com/@NinthHourFilms/about and ask for it to be deleted. We confirm and delete it within 30 days.

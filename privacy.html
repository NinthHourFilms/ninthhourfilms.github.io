AI CINEMA STUDIO

Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how AI Cinema Studio, the private desktop production tool of Ninth Hour Films ("we"), uses information when it is connected to Google services such as YouTube and Google Drive.

Who uses the application

AI Cinema Studio is used only by the Ninth Hour Films team on our own computer, with our own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What information the application accesses

  • YouTube: to upload our own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and publishing times, and to read our own channel's analytics and comments so we can improve our films.
  • Google Drive: only the backup files the application itself creates (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

How the information is used and stored

  • Information is used only to produce, publish and improve Ninth Hour Films content.
  • It is stored on our own computer and in our own Google Drive backups. Sign-in tokens are kept in the Windows secure credential store.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • When the application sends text or images to an AI service to help make a film, it sends only our own production material, never Google user data received through these permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AI Cinema Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us through the Ninth Hour Films YouTube channel at youtube.com/@NinthHourFilms/about.
